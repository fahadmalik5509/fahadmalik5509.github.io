Our Story
"Born in the golden hour between dusk and desire."

LUME was born from the belief that Pakistani women deserve jewelry that reflects their depth — not just their celebrations. Rooted in Mughal craft traditions and reimagined with a Gen Z eye, every piece is a bridge between then and now.

We work with artisans in Lahore's old city quarters, where families have passed down goldsmithing for generations. Their hands. Our vision.
